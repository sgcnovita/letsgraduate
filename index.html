How the quiz works:
• Questions remain in their original order.
• Choose any question range, such as Q1–20 or Q20–25.
• Check marks your answer and shows mistakes.
• Next skips the question temporarily.
• Skipped questions can be accessed from the question list.
• Capitalisation, punctuation and excessive spaces are ignored.
• Answers are checked word by word.
• Wrong questions are remembered for revision.
